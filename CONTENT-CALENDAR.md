## 2. Platform Strategy

### Platform-Content Matrix

| Platform | Primary Content Types | Posting Frequency | Best Times (PST) | Audience | Goal |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|-------------------|----------|------|
| **LinkedIn** | 🎓 Educational, 💰 Lab explainers, ⭐ Testimonials | 1x/day | 8:00–10:00 AM | B2B founders, service pros | Thought leadership, warm leads |
| **Instagram Reels** | 🎬 BTS, 🎓 Short clips, ⭐ Testimonials | 1-2x/day | 12:00 PM, 6:00 PM | Broad audience, local biz | Brand awareness, DM leads |
| **Instagram Stories** | 🎬 BTS, polls, behind-the-scenes | 3-5x/day | Throughout day | Existing followers | Engagement, community |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 🎓 Educational hooks, 💰 VSL clips | 1x/day | 2:00 PM | Search-driven discovery | SEO, evergreen traffic |
| **YouTube Long-form** | 💰 Full VSL, ⭐ Full testimonials, 🎙️ Podcast | 1x/week | 2:00 PM (Tuesday) | High-intent viewers | Deep trust, conversion |
| **TikTok** | 🎓 Repurposed reels, 🎬 BTS, trending overlays | 1-2x/day | 10:00 AM, 7:00 PM | Younger founders, viral reach | Discovery, brand |
| **Facebook** | 📢 Mirna ads, 🎓 Community content, ⭐ Testimonials | 1x/day | 9:00 AM | Local market, warm audiences | Retargeting, community |

### Platform-Specific Guidelines

**LinkedIn (Hiram's Voice)**
- Write in first person — founder POV
- Lead with a bold statement or contrarian take
- No hashtag spam (3-5 max, at end)
- Tag relevant people when appropriate
- Native video uploads only (no YouTube links)
- Long-form captions (LinkedIn rewards text + video combos)

**Instagram**
- Reels: 15-60 seconds, vertical, caption on screen
- Stories: Raw, unpolished, polls/questions for engagement
- Feed: Reserved for high-production pieces (Final Videos)
- Use location tags (Las Vegas)
- Hashtag strategy: mix of niche (#foundertips) and broad (#entrepreneurlife)

**YouTube**
- Shorts: Under 60 seconds, strong hook in first 3 seconds
- Long-form: Thumbnail + title optimization, chapters, end screens
- Descriptions with timestamps, links, CTAs
- SEO: target "founder video strategy," "business video content," "VSL examples"

**TikTok**
- Repurpose IG Reels directly
- Add trending sounds when relevant (don't force it)
- Use TikTok-native captions/text overlays
- Engage in comments — algorithm rewards interaction

**Facebook**
- Mirna-fronted ads run here (primary paid platform)
- Community/group content for organic
- Boost top-performing organic posts
- Link to assessment in every post

## 3. 30-Day Content Calendar

### Content Cadence Rules

1. **Never post the same content type 3 days in a row** on any platform
2. **Every post includes a CTA** — one of:
   - 🔗 "Take the free assessment → podlablv.com/assessment"
   - 📞 "Book a Foundation Call → Calendly link"
   - 💬 "DM us 'GROW' for details"
   - 🌐 "See our work → podlablv.com"
3. **Edited folder = primary organic content** (ready to post, no editing needed)
4. **Final Videos folder = paid ad creatives** (reserve for boosted/sponsored)
5. **BTS content goes on Stories + Reels** (authenticity plays)
6. **Cross-post strategically** — same video, different caption per platform

```
├── → TikTok (native upload, trending sound overlay option)
├── → LinkedIn Video (with long-form caption)
├── → Facebook Video (with community caption)
├── → Instagram Story (15-sec highlight + poll)
└── → Twitter/X clip (if account exists — 30-sec cut with hook)
```

### Batch Workflow (Weekly)
1. **Sunday evening:** Plan Monday–Friday content (select assets from this calendar)
2. **Monday morning:** Write all captions for the week using templates above
3. **Monday–Tuesday:** Schedule posts (use Buffer free tier for LinkedIn; native scheduling for IG/FB)
4. **Daily:** Post to TikTok manually (algorithm favors native posting)
5. **Daily:** 15 min engagement — respond to comments, DMs, engage with target accounts
6. **Friday:** Review week's analytics — what performed, what didn't, adjust next week
